RoundPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RoundPilot ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the RoundPilot: Interval Timer Android application.

1. Information stored on your device

RoundPilot stores app settings, timer configurations, presets, and other information required for the app to function locally on your Android device. This information is used to provide the timer, presets, settings, sound, voice and related app features.

RoundPilot does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, phone number, or other personal profile information in order to use the app.

2. Analytics

RoundPilot uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve reliability, usability, and future features.

Analytics may process information associated with app usage, such as events, interaction patterns, app version, device and operating-system information, and identifiers used by Firebase to distinguish app installations or sessions. The specific data collected depends on the Firebase services and configuration used by the app.

Examples of events recorded by RoundPilot may include starting, pausing, resuming, skipping, or stopping a workout, completing a workout, and using certain app features and settings.

3. Crash reports

RoundPilot uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect, investigate, and fix crashes and other technical problems.

Crashlytics may automatically collect information such as crash stack traces, relevant application state, device metadata, application version information, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. When Google Analytics for Firebase is used, breadcrumb information may also help show user actions immediately before a crash.

4. How information is used

Information processed by RoundPilot and its service providers is used to:

  • provide and maintain the app;
  • save and restore app settings and timer presets;
  • understand app usage and improve the user experience;
  • detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical issues;
  • monitor app stability and improve future versions.

5. Service providers

RoundPilot uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Information processed through these services is handled in accordance with Google's applicable policies and documentation.

6. Data retention

Information stored locally by RoundPilot remains on your device until it is removed by you or by the Android operating system or app data is cleared.

Data processed by Firebase services is retained according to the applicable Firebase service retention policies and configurations.

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used to operate and improve RoundPilot. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

RoundPilot is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children as a primary purpose of the app.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
